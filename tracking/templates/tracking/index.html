Shipment statuses

Know exactly where things stand

Every shipment moves through the same real stages, tracked from the moment a label is made to the moment it's signed for.

Label Created

A shipping label is generated and the package is ready for its first scan.

Picked Up

Collected from the sender and on its way to the first hub.

Arrived at Facility

Checked in at a hub somewhere along the route.

Departed Facility

Left that hub, moving toward the next stop or the final destination.

Out for Delivery

Loaded onto the vehicle for final delivery, usually arriving within the day.

Delivered

Signed for, with the exact time and signer shown on the tracking page.

Delayed

Held up along the way. Staff can log the reason, and tracking resumes as soon as it moves again.
